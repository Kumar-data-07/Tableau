# 👥 HR Analytics Dashboard

### Analyzing Employee Data, Workforce Trends & HR Performance using Tableau

An interactive Tableau project designed to analyze employee data and provide insights into **workforce composition, employee demographics, job roles, salary distribution, and HR-related trends**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** transforms employee data into interactive Tableau visualizations to help understand workforce characteristics and identify important HR patterns.

The project analyzes employee information such as **department, job role, gender, age, salary, education, experience, and employment-related attributes**.

The dashboard provides HR teams and business stakeholders with an easy-to-understand view of the organization's workforce and supports **data-driven HR decision-making**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the overall **employee workforce structure**.
- Understand employee distribution across **departments and job roles**.
- Analyze **gender and demographic distribution**.
- Examine **salary distribution and compensation patterns**.
- Identify workforce trends based on **age and experience**.
- Compare employee characteristics across different departments.
- Build an interactive dashboard for **HR reporting and decision-making**.

# 🔍 Key Analysis Areas

### 1. Workforce Distribution

Analyzes how employees are distributed across different **departments and job roles**.

### 2. Employee Demographics

Examines workforce composition based on **gender and age groups**.

### 3. Salary Analysis

Analyzes salary patterns across different **departments, job roles, and experience levels**.

### 4. Experience Analysis

Explores the relationship between **employee experience and compensation**.

### 5. Department Analysis

Compares departments based on **employee count, salary, experience, and other workforce characteristics**.

---

# 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard helps identify:

- Departments with the **largest employee populations**.
- Job roles with higher concentrations of employees.
- Differences in **workforce composition** across departments.
- Salary differences between **job roles and experience levels**.
- Distribution of employees across different **age and experience groups**.
- Workforce patterns that can support **HR planning and decision-making**.
