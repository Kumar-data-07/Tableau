# 🌍 Global Economic & Development Analysis

### Exploring Global GDP, Development, Trade, Inflation & Living Standards using Tableau

An interactive Tableau project that analyzes global economic and development indicators from **2000–2025** using World Bank data.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Global Economic & Development Analysis** project transforms World Bank data into interactive Tableau dashboards to explore global economic performance, development, trade, inflation, and living standards.

The project analyzes key indicators such as **GDP, GDP growth, GDP per capita, population, life expectancy, inflation, exports, and imports** across countries and years.

Three interactive dashboards were developed using Tableau to help users compare countries, identify trends, and understand global economic patterns.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze **global economic performance** across countries and years.
- Track trends in **GDP, GDP growth, and GDP per capita**.
- Compare **population and life expectancy** across countries.
- Examine **inflation and international trade growth**.
- Identify countries with strong **economic and development indicators**.
- Present complex data through **interactive Tableau dashboards**.
- Communicate insights through effective **data visualization and storytelling**.

## 📊 Dataset

The project uses **World Bank economic and development data** covering the period **2000–2025**.

### Key Indicators

- GDP (current US$)
- GDP growth (annual %)
- GDP per capita (current US$)
- Population, total
- Life expectancy at birth
- Inflation, consumer prices (annual %)
- Exports of goods and services (annual % growth)
- Imports of goods and services (annual % growth)
